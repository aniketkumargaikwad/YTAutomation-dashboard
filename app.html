YTAutomation Uploader

A private, single-owner tool that publishes the owner's own YouTube Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels on a schedule.

What it does

  • Uploads short gaming videos (YouTube Shorts) that the owner has produced, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Posts only to these channels, all owned by the developer:
    • Five Stars Deep
    • Meltdown On Stream
    • W In Chat Clips
    • Gully Wanted
  • Shows the owner a dashboard of what was posted and how it is performing.

Who can use it

Only the developer. There is no public sign-up, and no other person's Google account or YouTube data is ever requested or processed.

How it uses Google and YouTube

It uses the YouTube Data API with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload the owner's videos (videos.insert) and set their thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
  • youtube.readonly: confirm which of the owner's channels is signed in (channels.list), so a video never goes to the wrong channel.

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
